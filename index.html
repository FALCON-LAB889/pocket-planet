Flight Handbook Explorer guide ↓

Welcome aboard. Here's how to explore your little universe:

  1. Pick a planet. Tap an orbiting planet or its button below the solar system.
  2. Look around. Drag the 3D planet to see it from different sides.
  3. Make it yours. Change its name, colour, surface, rings, and moon.
  4. Keep your world. Tap “Save my planet” to keep your choices on this device.

Tip: Move your pointer over the solar system to pause the planets while choosing. On a tablet, use the planet buttons.
