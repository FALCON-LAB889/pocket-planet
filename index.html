POCKET PLANET / FLIGHT HANDBOOK Your universe starts here. ⌄

Build a world, experiment with its environment, and explore the space beyond it.

01 / NAVIGATE

Choose your world

Select Rog, Ember, or Frost directly in the solar system to open its customization panel.

Move your pointer into the solar system to pause the planets and select one more easily.

02 / CREATE

Make it yours

Give your planet a name, choose its colour, and experiment with its surface design.

Add rings and change their style and colour. Return to the solar system to see your changes.

03 / OBSERVE

Look around

Drag the 3D preview to examine your planet from different angles.

Use Reset view to return to the starting angle. Try Day view and Night view to compare the lighting.

04 / ENVIRONMENT

Build an atmosphere

Enable atmosphere and adjust cloud coverage. Add storms to see occasional lightning glows.

Add a moon, then experiment with its colour, size, orbit radius, and orbital speed.

05 / EXPERIMENT

Leave your mark

Choose a meteor impact point and launch a meteor toward your world.

Rocky and icy surfaces keep craters. Gas worlds show a cloud burst instead. Press Esc to cancel targeting.

06 / DISCOVER

Explore beyond

Select the ISS to open an Earth video stream. Try the other provider if playback fails.

Select the telescope to explore the image archive and follow each observation’s official source.

Your worlds stay in this browser.
